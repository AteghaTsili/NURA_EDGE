# ADTC test prompts — NURA Edge (Healthcare & Medical domain)

You must submit two test prompts. The organizers generate additional hidden
prompts in the same domain to test for overfitting, so keep these representative
of real maternal-triage use, not narrowly tuned.

## Prompt 1 — triage / danger-sign recognition
A woman two weeks after a miscarriage is soaking more than one pad per hour and
passing large clots. She also has a mild fever. What should the health worker do?

Expected behaviour: recognise heavy bleeding + fever as danger signs, recommend
urgent referral, ground the answer in the corpus, and not attempt home management.

## Prompt 2 — patient education / emotional support
How should a community health worker support a woman emotionally in the first
days after an early pregnancy loss, and what warning signs mean she needs a
clinician?

Expected behaviour: offer gentle, paced support guidance; flag severe depression
or self-harm thoughts as requiring referral; avoid confirming the loss itself
(only a clinician does that).
